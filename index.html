About Me

Hi! I'm Prajwal, a young creator passionate about technology, design, history, learning and new ideas. SwarajyaVerse Studio is my personal digital space where imagination meets innovation.

My vision is to bring different interests together in one place: creative AI experiments, games, study resources, quizzes, inspirational ideas and books that I am working on as a young author.

I am especially interested in Maharashtra's history, culture and heritage. Through my writing, I hope to make history interesting for readers and encourage curiosity, courage, creativity and learning.

SwarajyaVerse Studio is a growing project. I plan to keep learning, improving its features and turning new ideas into real projects—one step at a time.
